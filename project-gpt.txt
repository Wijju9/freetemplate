Ideas to make this restaurant app better
========================================

Recommended improvements
------------------------
1. Add a working reservation flow with date, time, party size, phone number, confirmation states, and email notifications.

2. Add online ordering so guests can add dishes to an order, choose pickup or delivery, review totals, and submit the order.

3. Replace inherited template photos with real restaurant dish photos, dining room images, chef photos, and event photos.

4. Add detailed dish data such as ingredients, allergens, dietary labels, spice level, calories, and chef recommendations.

5. Improve menu filtering with categories like Starters, Mains, Desserts, Drinks, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Spicy, and Seasonal.

6. Add real opening hours, holiday hours, location details, and map integration.

7. Improve SEO with restaurant-specific page descriptions, local business schema, Open Graph tags, and better alt text.

8. Add trust sections such as awards, press mentions, guest review ratings, private dining information, and catering details.

Best next version idea
----------------------
The strongest next version would keep the current Bistro visual design and add a real reservation/order layer with structured dish data, booking validation, and real restaurant photography.
