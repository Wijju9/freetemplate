Restaurant app conversion idea
==============================

Goal
----
Convert the current organic food template into a restaurant website/app while keeping the existing Bootstrap layout, responsive design, carousel, feature cards, testimonials, blog/news, and contact structure.

New restaurant concept
----------------------
The site can become a modern restaurant app for a fresh, healthy, farm-to-table restaurant. The current green/orange food branding can still work well, but the content should shift from selling grocery products to promoting dishes, dining experience, reservations, and online orders.

Page conversion plan
--------------------
1. Home page
   - Replace the organic grocery hero with restaurant messaging such as fresh meals, chef specials, seasonal menu, and table booking.
   - Change Products and Services buttons to View Menu and Book a Table.
   - Add sections for signature dishes, today specials, chef story, opening hours, reviews, and reservation call-to-action.

2. About page
   - Convert it into the restaurant story page.
   - Explain the chef, food philosophy, local ingredients, atmosphere, and hospitality values.

3. Product page
   - Convert it into a Menu page.
   - Product cards become dish cards with dish name, price, ingredients, dietary labels, spice level, and Add to Order button.
   - Tabs can become Starters, Main Course, Desserts, Drinks, Specials, and Vegan.

4. Feature page
   - Convert features into restaurant strengths such as Fresh Ingredients, Chef-Crafted Recipes, Fast Delivery, Private Dining, Catering, and Family Friendly.

5. Blog page
   - Convert to News, Recipes, Events, or Chef Journal.
   - Use it for seasonal announcements, food stories, special events, and offers.

6. Testimonial page
   - Keep it as guest reviews.
   - Add review sources, ratings, and customer dining photos if available.

7. Contact page
   - Convert into Contact and Reservation page.
   - Add address, phone, email, opening hours, Google Map, reservation form, party size, date/time picker idea, and special request field.

8. 404 page
   - Keep it but rewrite the message in restaurant tone, for example "This table is not available" or "This dish is off the menu".

Restaurant-specific features to add later
-----------------------------------------
- Online menu browsing.
- Table reservation form.
- Online ordering cart.
- Pickup/delivery option.
- Daily specials section.
- Opening hours and holiday hours.
- Location map.
- Gallery of food and dining space.
- Chef/team section.
- Catering/private event inquiry.
- Customer reviews with star ratings.
- Dietary filters such as vegan, gluten-free, spicy, and chef recommended.

Design direction
----------------
Use warmer food photography, restaurant interior images, dish closeups, and more appetizing call-to-action text. Keep the Bootstrap structure but adjust content, imagery, icons, and labels so the visitor immediately understands this is a restaurant, not a grocery store.

Important note
--------------
